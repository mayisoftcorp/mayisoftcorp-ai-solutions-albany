What agentic AI means for SMBs

Agentic AI is not just a chatbot. It is a workflow layer where AI systems help complete multi-step business tasks, coordinate information, and hand work back to humans when judgment is needed.

Where agentic AI creates value

Sales follow-up, intake, support triage, quote preparation, document processing, internal knowledge retrieval, scheduling, CRM updates, and operations reporting.

Safe implementation approach

Start narrow, keep humans in the loop, define escalation rules, log outputs, measure outcomes, and expand only after the workflow proves useful.
